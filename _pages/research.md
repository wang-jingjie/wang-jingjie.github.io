My research focuses on **human factors and ergonomics in complex, safety-critical systems**, with applications in **human-AI interaction, transportation, emergency evacuation, and healthcare**.

Across these domains, I use **behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and data-driven analysis** to understand human decision-making and human-technology interaction, with the broader goal of designing safer, more adaptive, and human-centered systems.

---

## 🚗 Human-AI Interaction & Transportation

My current research at Purdue University examines how people interact and collaborate with increasingly intelligent transportation systems.

I am particularly interested in **human-AI teaming, trust, transparency, explainability, and decision-making** in automated and AI-enabled vehicles.

## 🚶 Pedestrian Dynamics, Evacuation & Safety

My research in this area examines how **individual and group behavior shapes pedestrian safety and evacuation performance**.

I have used **agent-based simulation, social-force modeling, and behavioral data analysis** to study assistance behavior, pedestrian movement, and evacuation dynamics.

### 📚 Selected Publications

🏥 **Impact of Assistance Behavior on Evacuation Efficiency in High-Rise Hospital Inpatient Buildings: An Agent-Based Simulation Study**  
Jingjie Wang, Y. Luo, X. Zhou, W. Zhao, Y. Lei, & W. Xie  
*Journal of Building Engineering*, 2025  
🔗 [View Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobe.2025.113780)

🚶 **A Novel Gait-Based Bipedal Social Force Model for Simulating Pedestrian Dynamics on Inclined Surfaces**  
W. Xie, C. Xuan, Jingjie Wang, E. W. M. Lee, & B. Tian  
*Safety Science*, 2026  
🔗 [View Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2026.107119)

## 🧠 Human-Centered AI, Healthcare & Learning

I also collaborate on interdisciplinary research examining how **AI and emerging technologies can support healthcare, emotional well-being, and learning**.

These projects explore not only system performance, but also how people **perceive, trust, understand, and use emerging technologies** in human-centered contexts.
