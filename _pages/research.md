My research focuses on **human factors and ergonomics in complex, safety-critical systems**, with particular interests in **human-AI interaction, transportation, emergency evacuation, and healthcare**.

Across these domains, I use **behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and data-driven analysis** to understand how people interact with intelligent technologies and make decisions under uncertainty, with the broader goal of designing safer, more adaptive, and human-centered systems.

---

## Human-AI Interaction & Transportation

My current research at Purdue University examines how people interact and collaborate with increasingly intelligent transportation systems.

I am particularly interested in **human-AI teaming, trust, transparency, explainability, and decision-making** in automated and AI-enabled vehicles. My work includes research on automated-vehicle takeovers, large language models in driving contexts, and conceptual frameworks for human-vehicle teaming.

### Selected Work

**Tool, Teammate, or Rival? A Systematic Review of Evolving Human-AI Roles in Agentic Transportation**  
Jingjie Wang, Q. Wang, G. Li, & B. J. Pitts  
*Under review, Frontiers in Computer Science*

**From Code to Collaboration: A Cognitive Agent Framework for Large Language Model (LLM)-Based Human-Vehicle Teaming**  
Jingjie Wang & B. J. Pitts  
*Proceedings of the Human Factors and Ergonomics Society Annual Meeting, 2026*

My undergraduate thesis also examined how driver experience influences takeover performance during **silent automation failures**, which helped shape my continuing interest in human performance in automated transportation.

## Pedestrian Dynamics, Evacuation & Safety

A second major area of my research examines **pedestrian behavior and emergency evacuation**, particularly how individual and group behavior influence safety and system performance.

My work has combined **agent-based modeling, social-force modeling, and naturalistic trajectory analysis** to study assistance behavior, pedestrian gait, collision avoidance, and interactions between pedestrians and non-motorized vehicles.

### Selected Publications

**Impact of assistance behavior on evacuation efficiency in high-rise hospital inpatient buildings: An agent-based simulation study**  
Jingjie Wang, Y. Luo, X. Zhou, W. Zhao, Y. Lei, & W. Xie

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobe.2025.113780)

W. Xie, C. Xuan, Jingjie Wang, E. W. M. Lee, & B. Tian

[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2026.107119)

These projects examine not only technical performance, but also how people understand, trust, and use AI-enabled systems.
