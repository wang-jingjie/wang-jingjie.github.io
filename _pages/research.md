My research focuses on **human factors and ergonomics in complex, safety-critical systems**, with applications in **human-AI interaction, transportation, emergency evacuation, and healthcare**.

I use **behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and data-driven analysis** to understand human decision-making and human-technology interaction, with the goal of designing safer, more adaptive, and human-centered systems.

---

## 🚗 Human-AI Interaction & Transportation

I study how people interact and collaborate with increasingly intelligent transportation systems, with particular interests in **human-AI teaming, trust, transparency, explainability, and decision-making**.

## 🚶 Pedestrian Dynamics, Evacuation & Safety

My research in this area examines how **individual and group behavior shapes pedestrian safety and evacuation performance**. I have used agent-based simulation, social-force modeling, and behavioral data to study assistance behavior, pedestrian movement, and evacuation dynamics.

### 📄 Publications

🏥 **Impact of Assistance Behavior on Evacuation Efficiency in High-Rise Hospital Inpatient Buildings: An Agent-Based Simulation Study**  
Jingjie Wang, Y. Luo, X. Zhou, W. Zhao, Y. Lei, & W. Xie  
*Journal of Building Engineering, 2025*  
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobe.2025.113780)

🚶 **A Novel Gait-Based Bipedal Social Force Model for Simulating Pedestrian Dynamics on Inclined Surfaces**  
W. Xie, C. Xuan, Jingjie Wang, E. W. M. Lee, & B. Tian  
*Safety Science, 2026*  
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ssci.2026.107119)
